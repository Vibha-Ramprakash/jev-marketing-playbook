# The Jev playbook for marketers

8 copy-paste prompts for Jev, TypeSafe's decision model, covering sales, SEO, ads and content. Plus 2 free tools.

**Read it here:** https://vibha-ramprakash.github.io/jev-marketing-playbook/

## What's inside
- `index.html`: the guide (8 use cases, each with a prompt and a step by step)
- `tools/lead-scorer/`: Lead X-Ray. Scores a lead list against your ideal customer, live in your browser. Works straight from the web page.
- `tools/link-audit/`: Internal link audit. Reads your whole site and plans the missing internal links. Runs on your computer (see its README).

## Editing the guide
The use cases live in `build.py`. Edit them there, then run `python3 build.py` to rebuild `index.html`.
